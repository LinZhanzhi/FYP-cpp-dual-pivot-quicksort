# Final Presentation Requirements

## Time Allocation
**Maximum Duration: 20 minutes**

The presentation should include:
1. **Presentation of Results and/or Products**
2. **Demonstration**
3. **Question and Answer**

---

## Assessment Rubrics

### Presentation (PILO 1) - 10%

| Grade | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| **Excellent (10%)** | Comprehensive and very clear logical presentation showing in-depth understanding. Excellent response to all questions. |
| **Good (8%)** | Clear and well-organized presentation demonstrating good understanding. Good response to most questions. |
| **Satisfactory (6%)** | Adequate presentation with reasonable structure. Satisfactory response to questions with minor gaps. |
| **Marginal (4%)** | Presentation lacks clarity or organization. Limited response to questions with noticeable gaps in understanding. |
| **Poor (0-2%)** | Presentation is unclear, disorganized, or incomplete. Unable to respond to questions adequately. |

---

## Presentation Tips
- Ensure slides are readable and well-designed
- Practice timing to stay within the 20-minute limit
- Prepare for potential Q&A questions
- Have a working demo ready with backup plans
- Focus on key achievements and technical highlights
