# Final Report Requirements

## 10.2 Interim/Final Report

### 10.2.1 Organization of the Reports

The following is an example illustrating the organization of your interim/final report:

1. **Cover page** (Appendix A)
2. **Abstract**
3. **Table of contents**
4. **List of tables and figures**
5. **Main body of text**
6. **References/Bibliography**
7. **Appendices** (if any)

#### Key Requirements:
- Each chapter should start with a new page
- Clearly divided into sections and sub-sections with appropriate headings and numberings
- The final report should be a document of **high professional standard**
- Should be **self-standing and coherent**, well organized and presentable
- Materials from previous deliverables (e.g., project proposal and interim report) should be **re-structured and integrated** to ensure completeness and readability

#### Content Requirements:
- Clear and explicit statement of the **problem** and **objectives** in beginning chapters
- Include a section on **what has been achieved (your contributions)** to help examiners assess your work
- Consult with supervisor for specific report requirements

### 10.2.2 Cover and Formatting Requirements of the Reports

#### Cover Page Must Include:
1. Title of the project
2. Student ID no. of the student
3. Programme stream
4. Name of project supervisor
5. Date

> **Privacy Note:** As students' reports will be kept by the Department for demonstration/sharing to other students to enhance their learning, please **do not put your name and other personal particulars** on the report, except the cover page.

#### Formatting Guidelines:

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| **Font Style** | Arial or Times New Roman, **12 point** |
| **Font (special)** | 10 point may be used for: mathematical formulas/equations, figure/table/diagram captions, Symbol font for Greek letters or special characters |
| **Line Spacing** | At least **1.5 spacing**, no more than double line-spaced |
| **Margins** | Left: **25mm**; Top, bottom, right: **20mm** |
| **Layout** | One column, single-sided or double-sided **A4** |
| **Pagination** | Sequential pagination throughout (including appendices); Page number on **top right margin**; Title page counted but bears no page number |
| **Length** | Normally not exceed **50 pages** (excluding preamble and appendices) |

> **Remember:** Quantity is no substitute for quality.
